## Causes

- **points-pointsize**: Point clouds: edge pixels within 0.06 px of a wide point's square edge differ. The points are drawn (HgiWebGPU emulates gl_PointSize for every point-list program, the only ones Storm gives the point_size role; the old gl_PointSize warning came from non-point programs, where hgiMetal ignores it too). Our squares match a CPU model of Metal's pixel-center rule to 10 px of 8,338; the native captures deviate from that model by about +/-0.03 px per point, unexplained by size rounding, subpixel snapping, a one-pixel minimum or quantized edges (release/wide-points analysis). Debt: `PLATFORM-DIVERGENCE(hgiWebGPU-wide-points)`. Owner: I-4 points (imaging-i2.md 2.2, 2.2.1). Evidence: testHdStBasicDrawing: every failing image has the same 105 over-tolerance pixels after release/wide-points (160 before: sub-pixel points were raised to one pixel, and the point-cloud angles were accumulated in double where unitTestDelegate.cpp:1779 uses float), all inside the three point clouds at the right of the image (x 538-627, y 155-329 at 640x480); the meshes match. testUsdImagingGLPoints (MSAA disks): native disk centers sit 0.02-0.04 px off ours, where ours are exactly on the projected center. Nov 11, 2026 (macOS 27.3): the lighting_lv1 images differ on 105 px where they differed on 107 (the 105 over tolerance unchanged); d48515c31a rerun the same day gives 105 too, so the 2 one-level pixels went with the OS update, not the library.
- **wire-and-points**: Wire reprs: the triangle fill-mode-line emulation interpolates along the line, so edge pixels near the half-pixel threshold differ and refined grids on pixel boundaries draw solid; plus the point clouds of points-pointsize. Debt: `PLATFORM-DIVERGENCE(hgiWebGPU-polygon-mode-line)`, `PLATFORM-DIVERGENCE(hgiWebGPU-wide-points)`. Owner: I-4 (imaging-i2.md 2.2, triangle fill mode lines). Evidence: testHdStBasicDrawing wire/wireOnSurf images: about 100 px over the 160 px of the point clouds; refinedWire/refinedWireOnSurf: about 2,700-3,300 px, the solid refined grid edges imaging-i2.md 2.2 describes.

Owner: the suite's runner; rerun the suite (run.mjs --suites <id>) before reading anything into it.
